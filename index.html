What a normal website CANNOT silently read

Browsers have security boundaries. These things require explicit permission or are simply not accessible to web pages at all.

  • Phone number & installed appsNot exposed. A website cannot read your phone number or list your installed apps.
  • Contacts, files & call historyNot a web API. Those pop-ups were fake — no such browser permission exists. Only native apps with OS permission can read these.
  • Device name & serial numberNot provided to websites. At most, a browser shares broad platform hints.
  • Exact CPU model & full RAMBrowsers share coarse estimates only (threads, approximate GB) — not a hardware inventory.
  • Location, camera & microphoneThese require an explicit browser permission prompt. You can always deny or revoke it.

What a website might actually learn

Some information is shared as part of normal browsing; sensitive features require your explicit consent.

  • Your IP addressVisible to the website's server. May indicate a rough region or internet provider.
  • Browser & device fingerprintBrowser settings combined can be used to track visits. Privacy extensions can help.
  • LocationRequires your permission. Never approve it for a site without a clear reason.
  • Camera & microphoneMust request permission. A suspicious offer page has no good reason to ask.
  • What you typePasswords, card numbers, verification codes — fake forms exist to steal these.
  • Your interactionsA site tracks clicks, scrolls, and navigation on its own pages.

🛡️ Pause before you trust a message

  • Urgency and big rewards are classic manipulation tactics — slow down.
  • Check the real URL carefully. Scammers use look-alike domains (e.g. paypa1.com).
  • Never grant camera, microphone, or location to a page you didn't intentionally open.
  • No browser can read your contacts, files, or call history from a web page — that claim is always a lie.
  • Don't share passwords, card details, or verification codes through an unexpected link.
  • If something feels off, close the tab and go directly to the real website.
